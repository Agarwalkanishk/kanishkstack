A nutrition app that turns a photo of a food label into something useful. It reads the label with OCR, pulls calories and macros, and tracks meals against a daily target.

  • OCR pipeline extracts nutrition values from label photos, with rule-based validation before anything is stored.
  • Calculates calorie and macronutrient targets from user details, then compares each logged meal against them.
  • Full-stack build: Next.js and TypeScript on the front, PostgreSQL for users, meals, and targets.
